BEHIND THE EXPERIENCE

About this site

My portfolio, set in a 3D version of Adirondak Loj.

This scene is a walkable interpretation of the Adirondak Loj trailhead. The work board, project notebook, and personal photos are additions for this portfolio. The 3D clearing is an artistic reconstruction, not a scan or Google Street View.

Movement and collision run in Rust, compiled to WebAssembly. Three.js draws the scene. Forest sound is synthesized, not a field recording.

Photography & references

Generic forest environment and scanned floor/bark materials from Poly Haven (CC0). These materials are not photographs of the Loj.

The register shelter is modeled with reference to WAMC’s photograph of the Loj trail register. That photograph is not reproduced here.
