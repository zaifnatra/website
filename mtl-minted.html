Huzaifa Fareed / Project notes

mtl minted.

A way for artists to mint and tokenize their creative identity on Solana.

We won Best Use of Solana at McHacks 13, and our team won a US$1,000 microgrant for mtl minted.

What I built

I’m Huzaifa Fareed. My contribution was the Rust and Anchor programs behind minting, transfers, and transaction history. The project brings those pieces together around artists’ creative identities on Solana.

Recognition & funding

  • McHacks 13: Best Use of Solana winner.
  • Microgrant: US$1,000 awarded to our team for mtl minted.
