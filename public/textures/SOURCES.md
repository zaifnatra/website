# Environmental materials

All assets below are CC0 from Poly Haven: https://polyhaven.com/license

- `forest-slope.jpg`: Forest Slope environment, https://polyhaven.com/a/forest_slope. Downloaded tonemapped JPG and resized to 4096 pixels wide. This is a generic forest panorama and is NOT a photograph of Adirondak Loj.
- `ground-color.jpg`, `ground-normal.jpg`: Forest Floor, https://polyhaven.com/a/forest_floor. 1K JPEG diffuse and OpenGL normal maps, recompressed for browser delivery.
- `bark-color.jpg`, `bark-normal.jpg`: Bark Brown 02, https://polyhaven.com/a/bark_brown_02. 1K JPEG diffuse and OpenGL normal maps, recompressed for browser delivery.

These materials decorate an authored reconstruction. They do not establish the actual topography, vegetation placement, or structure dimensions of the Loj.
